Projected vs on pace

Projected = rax so far + each regular-season game his team has left × his rate a game × the chance he plays it × the opponent × home or away. His rate starts from last season (or the league average for a newcomer) and moves toward what he's doing this season the more games he plays, recent games counting most. The chance he plays comes from how many of his team's games he's played, pulled toward a typical regular (rest days, rotation, cup games). Injured reserve: no more games; "Out": the next one skipped. Every setting was tuned by replaying last season and checking against what players actually made.

On pace = rax so far + his rax a game played × his team's regular-season games left. No missed games, no opponents - just his rate.

FC: every game counts on Real (league, cups, Europe, internationals). Games left = how often his club has played so far × the days left to its league's last day (winter break taken out); no opponent adjustment, and future international games aren't counted.

What the numbers mean

Now - rax he has so far this regular season · games he's played · rax a game.

Left - his team's regular-season games left · projected rax a game · next opponent · team's games in all.

Projected - rax by the end of the regular season: what he has now plus the games left, counting the chance he misses games and who he plays.

On pace - rax by the end of the regular season if he keeps his rax a game for every game left, never missing one.

proj −125 - Projected minus On pace: the projection is 125 lower (missed games, tougher opponents, a hot start cooling off). + means higher.
