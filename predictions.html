What EV means

EV (expected value) is how much a bet returns on average if you could make it over and over. +10% EV means every 100 rax you put on it comes back as 110 rax on average. Any single bet still wins or loses - EV only shows up over many bets.

Step 1 - Real's price

On a Real game market each side has a %. That % is what you pay: a side at 40% costs 40 to win 100 (your 40 back plus 60). The lower the %, the bigger the payout and the less likely it is to hit.

Step 2 - The fair price

Sportsbooks build a margin into their odds (both sides add up to more than 100%). We take that margin out of each book so its two sides add up to exactly 100%, then average the books: Pinnacle (counts double), BetMGM, Novig, DraftKings, FanDuel, Kalshi and Caesars. That average is the fair % - the best guess at the real chance. It shows under each team's name. Polymarket shows for comparison but isn't in the average.

Step 3 - The edge

EV = fair % ÷ Real's % - 1. If a team is 50% fair but Real sells it at 40%, EV = 50 ÷ 40 - 1 = +25%. If Real's % is higher than fair, EV is negative and you skip it.

Step 4 - Units (how much to bet)

A unit (u) is 1% of your rax. Enter your rax at the top and every recommendation shows the exact rax to put on it. Sizes come from the Kelly formula, which sizes a bet by how big the edge is compared with how much you'd lose: Kelly % = (fair - Real) ÷ (1 - Real). We bet a tenth of that (much safer than full Kelly), so a 10% Kelly bet = 1u. Sizes run from 0.25u to 2u, rounded to the nearest 0.25u.

Long shots are capped because they lose most of the time even with a big edge: under 20% on Real it's 0.5u max, under 35% 1u, under 50% 1.5u, and favorites can go up to 2u.

Example with 5,000 rax: 1u = 50 rax. A favorite at 60% with a 70% fair price is Kelly 25% → 2u → 100 rax. An underdog at 15% with a 20% fair price is Kelly 6% → capped 0.5u → 25 rax.

Step 5 - When not to trust it

Thin odds: during a game, if fewer than 3 books have a line, or the newest line is over a minute old, the EV is greyed out and gets no unit size - books freeze their lines in blowouts and late in games, so a huge EV there is usually just an old line. Open Book breakdown to see every book's % and how old its line is.

Hidden games: games where a side's fair % is under 7% (basically decided) are hidden - tap Show all to see them. Locked markets are always hidden.

Step 6 - Staying disciplined

Bet the size it says, not more because you feel sure. Big edges on long shots still lose most of the time; the point is that over a few hundred bets the +EV ones come out ahead. Real's prices update every 10 seconds and live sportsbook odds every 20 seconds, so check the timers at the top before you bet.
