What EV means

EV (expected value) is how much a bet returns on average if you could make it over and over. +10% EV means every 100 rax you put on it comes back as 110 rax on average. Any single bet still wins or loses - EV only shows up over many bets.

Step 1 - Real's price

On a Real game market each side has a %. That % is what you pay: a side at 40% costs 40 to win 100 (your 40 back plus 60). The lower the %, the bigger the payout and the less likely it is to hit.

Step 2 - The fair price

Sportsbooks build a margin into their odds (both sides add up to more than 100%). We take that margin out of each book so its two sides add up to exactly 100%, then average the books: Pinnacle (counts double, before the game only - its live line lags), BetMGM, Novig, DraftKings, FanDuel, Kalshi and Caesars. That average is the fair % - the best guess at the real chance. It shows under each team's name. Polymarket shows for comparison but isn't in the average.

Step 3 - The edge

EV = fair % ÷ Real's % - 1. If a team is 50% fair but Real sells it at 40%, EV = 50 ÷ 40 - 1 = +25%. If Real's % is higher than fair, EV is negative and you skip it.

Step 4 - Units (how much to bet)

A unit (u) is 1% of your rax. Enter your rax at the top and every recommendation shows the exact rax to put on it. Sizes come from the Kelly formula, which sizes a bet by how big the edge is compared with how much you'd lose: Kelly % = (fair - Real) ÷ (1 - Real). We bet a tenth of that (much safer than full Kelly), so a 10% Kelly bet = 1u. Sizes run from 0.5u to 2u in half-unit steps (rounded down).

Long shots are capped because they lose most of the time even with a big edge: under 20% on Real it's 0.5u max, under 35% 1u, under 50% 1.5u, and favorites can go up to 2u.

Example with 5,000 rax: 1u = 50 rax. A favorite at 60% with a 70% fair price is Kelly 25% → 2u → 100 rax. An underdog at 15% with a 20% fair price is Kelly 6% → capped 0.5u → 25 rax.

Placing a bet

Every side shows a unit ladder, like 0.5u under 50% · 1u under 47% · 2u under 44%: the size to bet at each Real price, from the same rule as above. Live prices swing between refreshes, so if Real jumps to 46% you already know it's a 1u bet without waiting. The size the current price earns is lit green. +EV up to X% is the highest price that's still worth a bet at all; above it, skip. Once you buy, tap Bet placed, put in the Real % you actually got and the rax you bet (both filled in for you), and tap Enter: the game leaves Markets and shows in My bets. Change the amount if you put in something different; the payout (amount ÷ the price you paid) updates with it. The payout can be changed too if Real paid something different (clear it to go back to auto). When the market settles on Real it's marked Won or Lost for you; you can still tap Won or Lost yourself.

Arbitrage (locking in profit)

Once you hold a side, Real's price often swings the other way during a game. If you then buy the other side so both sides pay the same, you win the same amount whichever team wins. Example: 300 rax on a team at 30% pays 1,000. If the other side drops to 55%, buying it for 570 rax also pays 1,000 - you've staked 870 total and get 1,000 back either way: +130 locked (15%). The Arbitrage tab works this out for every open bet, adds slippage worked out per hedge (how far your buy pushes Real's price for that market's volume, plus how much the line has been swinging; you can add an extra cushion), and only calls it ready at 10%+ guaranteed profit. It also shows the price the other side has to reach. After you buy, tap Hedge placed and it shows as locked.

Step 5 - When not to trust it

Thin odds: during a game, if fewer than 3 books have a line, or the newest line is over a minute old, the EV is greyed out and gets no unit size - books freeze their lines in blowouts and late in games, so a huge EV there is usually just an old line. Open Book breakdown to see every book's % and how old its line is.

Hidden games: the +EV tab shows only games with a bet to make, best EV first, and leaves out games where a side's fair % is under 7% (basically decided). All shows every open market. Locked markets are always hidden.

Step 6 - Staying disciplined

Bet the size it says, not more because you feel sure. Big edges on long shots still lose most of the time; the point is that over a few hundred bets the +EV ones come out ahead. Real's prices update every 10 seconds and live sportsbook odds every 20 seconds, so check the timers at the top before you bet.
