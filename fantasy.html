Underdog slips from the picks with an edge, one pick per player. Tap a pick on Picks to lock it into every slip.

Fair %. Each sportsbook's over/under is de-vigged (the book's margin removed), then moved to Underdog's or Kalshi's line with a normal curve sized to the stat. The books are weighted: Pinnacle 2, FanDuel 1.5, Novig 1.2, the rest 1. A pick needs at least 2 books.

Underdog. Every pick is compared to the breakeven of the slip type picked in Settings. For example, a 5-pick Flex needs about 54% a pick to break even with the default payouts. Slips are scored with the full payout table, partial hits included.

Kalshi. EV = fair % / (price + Kalshi's fee) - 1. The fee is taken as 7% x price x (1 - price) per contract.

Data. The Odds API, pregame only. Each game refreshes as often as 30,000 credits a day allows (never faster than 2 min). Pulls only run while this page was opened in the last 3 hours.

Underdog's payouts change. Check them in the app and set them in Settings, since the edges depend on them.
