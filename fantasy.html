Best Underdog slips, one pick per player. Picks you've checked go into every slip.

Fair %. Each sportsbook's over/under is de-vigged (the book's margin removed), then moved to Underdog's or Kalshi's line with a normal curve sized to the stat. The books are weighted: Pinnacle 2, FanDuel 1.5, Novig 1.2, the rest 1. A pick needs at least 2 books whose line is close to the app's line (within 3/4 of a spread), and fair % is capped at 95% either way.

Underdog. Every pick is compared to the breakeven of the slip type picked in Settings. For example, a 5-pick Flex needs about 54% a pick to break even with the default payouts. Slips are scored with the full payout table, partial hits included.

Kalshi. EV = fair % / (price + Kalshi's fee) - 1. The fee is taken as 7% x price x (1 - price) per contract.

Data. The Odds API, pregame only, and only when you tap Pull props. One pull covers every NBA and NFL game starting in the next 30 hours, at about 1 credit per stat per game (roughly 100-150 credits a pull on a full slate). Hard cap: 30,000 credits a day.

Underdog's payouts change. Check them in the app and set them in Settings, since the edges depend on them.
