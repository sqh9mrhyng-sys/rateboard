How to read this

Rax is what a player's games have paid on the base rate this season; a pass's rarity multiplies it. Per game is total rax divided by games played - the fairest way to compare a player who has missed time with one who hasn't. Best is his biggest single game, a hint at how high his ceiling is when he hits.

For most sports the leaders by total rax and by rax per game are different lists: high-volume starters top the first, stars with fewer games the second. When you're choosing whom to build, look at both - and check injury news before boosting anyone.
