NHL on Real Sports

The NHL plays an 82-game regular season from October to April, usually three or four games a week per team.

What drives NHL rax

  • Top-line forwards who score and set up goals lead the rax lists; defensemen who run the power play aren't far behind.
  • Points come in bunches, so per-game averages are lumpy - judge players over 15-20 games, not a week.
  • Back-to-back nights sometimes mean a skater sits, and goalies rotate.
  • Steady ice time is the best predictor of steady rax.
