NFL on Real Sports

The NFL plays one game a week across a 17-game regular season, so every NFL pass gets about one payday a week - and each of those games matters. A healthy starter rarely misses a week except for his bye.

What drives NFL rax

  • Running backs and receivers who see a big share of their team's touches lead the rax lists; the very top quarterbacks follow.
  • With one game a week, a single injury or bye costs a whole week's earnings - check the injury report and bye weeks before boosting.
  • Thursday, Sunday and Monday games each count on their own day, so a booster used Thursday is gone for the weekend.
  • Because there are so few games, per-game averages settle slowly early in the season; lean on last season's numbers until about week 4.
