A guide to Real Sports cards

Real Sports is an app where you collect player passes for real games and earn rax when those players perform. This guide explains how the pieces fit together - ratings, rarities, packs, boosters, auctions and swaps - with the numbers the tools on this site use. It is written by and for the Rax Academy community, and the Real app is always the final word on any rule.

How rax is earned

Every game a player plays gets a performance rating from his stat line. When you hold a pass for that player, the game pays you rax: the better the game and the rarer your pass, the more you earn. A pass's rarity works as a multiplier, so the same game pays a Rare pass (4x) twice what a Common pass (2x) earns, and an Epic pass (10x) five times as much.

That is why most of the work on Real goes into two things: picking players who will play well and often, and building their passes up to higher rarities.

Rarities and the rating it takes to reach them

A pass starts at General. Each card you add from that player's packs adds its rating to the pass, and once the total crosses a threshold the pass moves up a rarity. These are the thresholds and multipliers the site's tools use:

Example. A player whose plays average 5 rating gives about 15 rating a pack (3 plays). Reaching Rare (80) takes about 6 packs; reaching Epic (180) takes about 12.

Packs

A player pack holds three of that player's plays, drawn from every play he has had that season. Every play in the pool is equally likely, so the chance of pulling a big game is simply how many big games he has had divided by how many plays there are. A player with a handful of huge games and few plays overall gives the best odds; a player with hundreds of average plays gives steady but small packs.

Team packs work the same way but draw from one team: three plays from that team plus one guaranteed performance from it, from games rated .5 or higher. Packs reset once a day, at 3am Alaska time (7am Eastern), and Real limits how many packs of one sport and season you can open in a day.

The Pack data page lists every player's plays and performances by rarity, with the odds of pulling each kind of card and a calculator for how many packs a pass needs to reach its next rarity.

Boosters

A booster raises what a pass earns for one game. On a General pass the booster's own multiplier is what you get. On a pass above General, the booster's multiplier is added to the pass's own: a Rare booster (10x) on a Common pass (2x) earns 12x for that game. Since boosters are limited, the best use is on players most likely to have a big game that day - usually healthy starters with high recent averages and a favorable matchup.

The Boosts & Buys page projects each player's rax for the day's games and suggests which booster goes on which pass.

Auctions

Passes can be sold in Real's auctions. Real keeps 10% of the final price, so a pass that sells for 3,000 rax pays the seller 2,700. When you price a pass, compare it against what similar passes of that player and rarity have actually sold for recently, and remember that a listing with no competition at the same time tends to sell for more.

Swaps

Because every card counts toward a pass's rating no matter whose pack it came from, collectors trade packs: you open a pack of a player someone else is building, and they open one of yours. On Rax Academy's Swaps you pick the passes you want, the passes you'd give, and the hours you're free; the site pairs you with a member who wants what you have and messages you both at your swap time. There is no money involved - just packs for packs.

One claim a day

A pass pays once per day. If a player plays twice in one day - a baseball doubleheader, for example - the day's rax is claimed once, not once per game. The OTD page breaks down every player's earnings day by day, by month and by season, so you can see which players pay steadily and which pay in bursts.

Tips that hold across every sport

  • Games played matter as much as quality. A good player who plays every day out-earns a great one who sits often.
  • Check injury news before boosting. A booster on a player who doesn't play is wasted.
  • Build a few passes high rather than many passes low. The multipliers jump at Epic and Legendary, so the last packs to cross a threshold are worth the most.
  • Look at pack pools before buying packs. Players with few plays and many big games give the best odds per pack.
  • Sell into demand. Late mornings and midday see the most auction bidding.
