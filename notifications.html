Push notifications

  1. Tap the 3 dots at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Tap View More.
  4. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  5. Don't change anything - just tap Add.
  6. Open the Rax Academy app from your Home Screen, tap Alerts, and turn notifications on.

Activate notifications

Turn on push notifications for this device above first - then choose which ones you want here.

Swaps

With a DM turned off you only get the notification - if it can't reach any of your devices, the DM goes anyway so you never miss a swap.

Boosts

A reminder to boost before the first game of the day. Pick how long before (up to 36 hours).

OTD claim

A daily reminder: "Claim your OTD - you have X today". X comes from your OTD breakdown for today's date. No notification on days you'd get nothing.

Bulk notifications

Brand-new listings only (just put up, not ones ending with bids), with a buy now well under the usual price for that player - from recent bulk sales, else the same rarity's.
