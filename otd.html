MLB targets

Dennis's 15 MLB pitcher passes, weighed against your calendar. For each: the level that makes the most profit over the window and pack price above (a new pass starts at 0 rating), what it adds after the 2-per-sport (+1 spare) claim limit, and which of your passes it pushes off - the ones it would replace on the days they clash. Clashes is how many of its dates already have two MLB claims of yours. Best order picks them one at a time, each worked out after the ones before, while they still make a profit.

Best upgrades

For each pass, the level that adds the most for what it costs. Extra a year is what your whole calendar gains after the 2-per-sport (+1 spare) claim limit, and after each pass's all-time cap on old seasons. Extra all-time is how much more cap room the upgrade opens (∞ at Iconic or on a live season). Packs come from pack data (average rating ×3 a pack, never under 10) at the price above; FC has no packs, so it's priced from the Rate Board: the best standing offer per rating for that player, or the median FC offer. Profit is what the upgrade earns over the window you pick, from today, minus its price; each pass shows its most profitable target. Tap a row for its calendar.
