What are swaps?

Swaps are the most efficient way on the app to grind a player.

How it works

You select a player you want, for example JSN. You find someone who wants a different player, for example Jahmyr Gibbs.

You buy a Jahmyr Gibbs pack, and the other user buys a JSN pack. You go to his profile and his collection and offer on the 3 JSN cards he just got. He goes to your profile and offers on the 3 Gibbs cards you just got.

What it costs

You "swap" packs. A swap typically costs 241 rax. For a legendary player pass you're paying 9,200 at the very most - all top tier players will profit that 9,200, or you can resell for more.

⚠️ WARNING PSA
If you use swaps in an auction group and in turn unintentionally scam someone through the website, you will be banned from the website and group. No warnings.

4. Who you want

Pick up to 5 passes you want cards bought for - each has to be one you own (scan in step 3 first). More picks means more people you can swap with; your first pick is tried first.

5. When you can be messaged

Pick up to 5 hours a day. The first one you tap is your primary, then 2nd, 3rd and so on - if two people want the same player, the one whose primary hour it is gets the swap. The number under each hour is how many people picked it. Shown in your time (). You only get matched with someone who picked at least one of the same hours, and you're both messaged on Real at that hour.
