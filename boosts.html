How this is worked out

Steady rax/game is his average with his best game left out, so one huge game can't carry him; buys rank on that and on how often he has a good game (CFB 20+, CBB 10+), and profits use it. Games left go by his team's games: a 31-game CBB / 12-game CFB regular season, less what the team has played. Postseason games (bowls, CFP) count in rax a game once played. Swap = new card's rax over his games left × the booster, minus 200 for the pack and the resell cost (everything your old card made past 100 rax). Keep = your card's rax over his games left × the booster (0 if he's out for the season). Replace only when swap beats keep.
