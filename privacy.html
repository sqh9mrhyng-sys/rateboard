Privacy policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information Rax Academy (raxacademy.app, "the site") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

Account. Your Real username and a password you choose for this site. Passwords are never stored as typed - only a scrambled fingerprint of them, which cannot be turned back into your password. Please don't reuse a password from anywhere else.

What you post. Buy offers on the board, swap choices (the passes you want or won't give, and your hours), ratings of swap partners, reports and feature requests.

Information collected when you use the site

Your Real cards. When you ask the site to read your passes, it reads the public card information Real shows for your username (which passes you own, their ratings and boosters) so the tools can work for you.

Usage. Which pages are opened and when, so we can keep the site fast and count visits. These counts do not show your name to anyone but the site's admin.

Browser storage. The site keeps your sign-in and a few settings (like the sport you picked) in your browser's local storage, so you stay signed in.

Advertising and cookies

The site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners show ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info. To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

How information is used

  • To run your account and the tools: the board, swaps, pack data, boosts and projections.
  • To match swaps and send swap messages to your Real account through the site's bot.
  • To keep the community fair: reports, ratings and the warning and strike system.
  • To keep the site working, fast and safe.

We do not sell your information.

Who can see what

Your username appears next to things you choose to share, like your board offers and your swap partners' swap pages. Other members can see your swap rating. Your password fingerprint, reports and account details are visible only to the site's admin.

Where data is kept

The site runs on Cloudflare. Your data is stored there and protected by Cloudflare's security.

Your choices

You can stop swaps or sign out at any time. To have your account and its data deleted, contact us (see below) from your Real account.

Children

The site is meant for people who use the Real Sports app under that app's own age rules. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? See the contact page.
